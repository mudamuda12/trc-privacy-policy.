Privacy Policy

This page explains how the TRC Discord bot handles information when you use its commands and features.

Effective date: November 22, 2026 Service: TRC Discord Bot
Important: This policy must match the bot's actual data practices. Before publishing, replace the contact placeholders and verify what is stored in the bot database, VPS logs, backups, and third-party services.

1. Introduction

The Rejected Citadel (TRC) is a multi-purpose Discord bot that provides server management, moderation, security, automation, and AI-assisted community features. This Privacy Policy describes the information TRC may process, why it is processed, and how users or server administrators can contact the operator with privacy requests.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on which features a Discord server enables, TRC may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed to operate commands and server settings.
  • Basic account details: usernames or display names when needed to show command responses, moderation logs, or member-related features.
  • Message data: message content when required to process prefix commands, AI chat requests, or enabled spam and mass-mention detection.
  • Server configuration: feature settings, selected log channels, language preferences, and temporary voice-channel configuration.
  • Feature activity: reminder settings and other data needed to provide a feature requested by a user or server administrator.

TRC is not intended to collect information unrelated to providing its Discord bot features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Respond to bot commands and interactions.
  • Provide Anti-Raid, Anti-Nuke, spam, and mass-mention protection when enabled.
  • Create and manage temporary voice channels.
  • Provide reminders, language settings, and server configuration.
  • Generate AI responses when a user chooses to interact with the AI feature.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

4. Storage and Retention

TRC may store configuration and operational records on its hosting infrastructure so that settings and supported features continue to work after a restart. The exact data retained depends on the feature used and the bot's current implementation.

Some information may be processed temporarily in memory. Operational logs and backups may also exist on the hosting infrastructure. The operator should define and follow an appropriate retention period for each type of stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

TRC uses Discord to provide its bot functionality. When a user chooses to use TRC's AI chat feature, the relevant prompt or message context may be sent to Groq to generate a response. The processing of that information is also subject to the third-party provider's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

TRC's operator should review the current data handling terms of each third-party service used by the bot. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information to the AI feature.

6. Sharing

TRC does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to deliver a feature, or disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

7. User Choices and Data Requests

Users can stop using TRC at any time. Server administrators can disable optional features or remove the bot from their server. Users and server administrators may contact the operator to ask what information is held about them or to request deletion, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

A request should identify the relevant Discord user ID and server, where applicable, and describe the data or feature concerned. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, or API keys.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by TRC. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

TRC is a general-purpose Discord bot and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe information has been processed inappropriately, contact the operator using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when TRC's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published. Continued use of TRC after an update means the updated policy is available for review; it does not replace any rights provided by applicable law.
